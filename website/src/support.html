Support

Updated November 8, 2026

Something not working, or an idea for Sapphire? Open an issue on GitHub, and say which device and OS version you use.

iOS beta

Join from the public TestFlight link, or through the waitlist on the home page. If the invite does not appear, check that TestFlight is installed and that you used the same Apple ID.

Android

Android is on the waitlist while the build is prepared. Leave your email on the home page and we will write when a build is ready.

Your vault

Sapphire opens a folder you choose. Deleting the app does not delete that folder. Keep a backup of anything you care about the same way you already back up your vault.
