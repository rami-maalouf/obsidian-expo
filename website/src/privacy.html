Privacy

Updated November 8, 2026

Sapphire is built so your notes stay yours. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics for your vault. It is not affiliated with Obsidian.

What stays on your device

Your vault is a folder you choose. Sapphire reads and writes those Markdown files in place. The developer cannot see them.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist on this site, we collect only your email address and whether you asked for iOS or Android, so we can notify you about the beta. That email is stored securely and is not sold or shared for advertising.

TestFlight

The iOS beta is distributed through Apple's TestFlight. Apple's privacy policy covers that install path.

App updates

Sapphire may check Expo's update service for fixes to the app itself. That request carries technical details such as the app version and platform, and none of your notes.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub.
